# Design brief — THE WORLD, Monday 23 November 2026

## Concept: "FRACTURE"

Monday 23 November 2026 was a day of fractures — things that were supposed to
hold together, splitting apart:

- The climate process fractured in Antalya: COP31 ended with **no deal**, the
  first collapse since Copenhagen 2009, because the final text said nothing
  about fossil fuels.
- The AI boom fractured: markets slid again across Asia, with Seoul down more
  than 5% and the week's losses wiping out the year's gains.
- The war fractured into new seas: a Russian warship sunk in Libya, a second
  Russian ship hit in the Baltic — the fighting now reaches far beyond the
  Black Sea.
- American politics fractured into a new balance: the Democrats' biggest
  House majority since 1992.

## Visual motif

One thin jagged crack runs beneath the masthead and reappears as a small
motif beside the lead story — the front page itself looks cracked. The mood
is sober and watchful, not sensational. Black ink on warm paper, one muted
brick-red accent for the crack and the lead kicker only.

## Layout (1024 × 1024)

- Masthead "THE WORLD" + dateline "Monday, 23 November 2026" + crack line.
- LEAD: COP31 collapse, big headline + deck + CSS illustration (empty
  negotiating hall / broken globe motif).
- Middle band, three stories: Russian warship sunk in Libya; AI sell-off
  spreads through Asia; Democrats' biggest House majority since 1992.
- Bottom strip "WORLD IN BRIEF": Houthis claim Saudi refinery strike;
  South Korea education minister resigns; Russian strike kills 10 in Dymer;
  FIFA to confirm UK as 2035 Women's World Cup host; Banten factory fire;
  Gaza contamination warning.
- Folio line: "THE WORLD — Monday, 23 November 2026".

## Rules

- Single self-contained HTML file, all CSS inline, no JavaScript, no external
  resources, system fonts only (Georgia serif headlines, system sans labels).
- Every fact verified from Monday 23 Nov 2026 reporting; nothing invented.
- Headlines in my own words; correct English spelling; legible at 300 px.
